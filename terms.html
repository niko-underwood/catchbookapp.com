2. Free tier

Logging catches is free and unlimited. Without a subscription you can view, edit, filter, and map your catches; use auto-filled conditions; and use iCloud backup and restore. The Dashboard shows card titles to free users, with the underlying data blurred until you subscribe. Current conditions on the Conditions tab are free; comparing them with your past catches, and GPX export, require Catch Book Pro.

Eligibility is decided by the App Store; generally one free trial per Apple Account. The trial length and the price that follows are shown in the app before you start.

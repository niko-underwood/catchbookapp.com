Restore purchases

Your Catch Book Pro subscription belongs to your Apple Account, so it usually turns on by itself on a new iPhone or after reinstalling. If it doesn’t:

  1. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.
  2. In Catch Book, tap the profile icon (top right) and open Settings > Catch Book Pro > Restore Purchases.
  3. If it says no subscription was found, check Settings app > [your name] > Subscriptions to see whether the subscription is active on that Apple Account.

iCloud backup and restore

Catch Book backs up your catches and photos to your own iCloud Drive automatically a few seconds after each change, and keeps dated backups so you can go back.

  • Turn it on: iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > iCloud Drive on, and make sure Catch Book is allowed to use iCloud Drive.
  • Check or back up now: in Catch Book, tap the profile icon (top right) > Settings > Backup & Restore. It shows where the backup is and when it last uploaded. Tap Back Up Now any time.
  • New iPhone or reinstall: on first launch Catch Book offers to Restore Your Catches. You can also go to Settings > Backup & Restore > Restore from Backup.
  • Brought back something you deleted? Restore from Backup lists dated backups. Pick one from before the delete; it shows what it will add, and it never creates duplicates.
  • Restoring is always free and never blocked.

Where backups are in the Files app

Open Files > Browse > iCloud Drive > Catch Book > Fish Tracker Backup. You’ll see FishTrackerBackup.json, a previous copy, and the Photos and History folders. On a Mac, look in Finder under iCloud Drive > Catch Book. (A new folder can take a few minutes to appear on other devices.)

Please don’t edit or rename these files; the app manages them.

Cancel a subscription

  1. Open the iPhone Settings app, tap [your name] > Subscriptions, then Catch Book Pro.
  2. Tap Cancel Subscription. (In Catch Book, Settings > Catch Book Pro > Manage Subscription opens the same screen.)

Free trial: to avoid being charged after a 7-day free trial, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Pro stays on until the trial ends.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for, and you keep all your catches after that. Dashboard insights, the wind & wave forecast, and GPX export then require Pro again. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com; refunds are handled by Apple.

Weather or tides not loading

  • Check your connection. Weather, tides, and water temperature need internet. Offline, you’ll see “Offline – enter environmental data manually”. Fill them in yourself or edit the catch later.
  • Check location. Conditions are looked up for the catch location. Allow location for Catch Book in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services, or enter coordinates manually.
  • Tides and water temperature come from the nearest NOAA station to the catch (U.S. coasts, Great Lakes, and territories), within about 100 miles. Elsewhere these fields stay empty. Some stations report tides but not water temperature. The app shows the station name and the tide state at the catch time.
  • Weather comes from Apple Weather. If it’s briefly unavailable, try again in a few minutes.
  • Waves (version 1.3) come from Open-Meteo’s marine model for the nearest open water, for catches from late 2021 on. Most inland lakes and rivers show “Not available.” Catches saved offline get their waves later from Settings > Update Conditions for Past Catches.

Free vs Pro

Logging catches is free and unlimited. Free also includes the map, filters, auto conditions (weather, tide, pressure, waves, moon phase), weight and Kept/Released, and iCloud backup and restore. Catch Book Pro unlocks the 7-day wind & wave forecast, Dashboard insights, the “Your best conditions” comparison on the Conditions tab, and GPX export. On the Dashboard and Conditions tabs, free users still see every card’s title; the numbers stay blurred until you upgrade. New subscribers can try Pro free for 7 days on either plan (one free trial per Apple Account); the app shows the trial and the price before you start.

Conditions tab

The Conditions tab shows the conditions where you are right now: the tide at the nearest NOAA station, moon phase, air and water temperature, barometric pressure and trend, wind, and (version 1.3) waves. Pull down to refresh. It needs location permission; if location is off, the tab explains how to turn it on. With Catch Book Pro, “Your best conditions” shows the range most of your catches came in for each condition (for all species or one), whether today matches, and an overall “Today matches X of N.” Each condition needs at least 5 logged catches that recorded it.

Wind & wave forecast (Pro, version 1.3)

On the Map, press and hold a spot on the water (or tap the wind button at the top left for the middle of the map) to drop an orange Forecast pin, then tap View Forecast. For a spot where you already have a catch, tap the catch’s pin and then Forecast next to Share GPX. The forecast covers 7 days, every 3 hours or hourly: wind average and gusts (mph) with direction, wave height with an estimated peak (about 1 wave in 10), wave period, and swell. It’s a computer-model forecast from Open-Meteo for the nearest open water, not a buoy reading, and not for navigation. On land you’ll see wind only. It needs an internet connection; free users see the Catch Book Pro screen when they tap it.

Export GPX

With Catch Book Pro you can share a single catch as a GPX waypoint from Catch Details, or export your whole log from Settings > Export (profile icon, top right). The files are GPX 1.1 waypoints suitable for Garmin ActiveCaptain, Navionics, and other chart apps. Free users see the paywall when they tap those buttons.

Tour and tips

On first launch, Catch Book offers a short tour of Log Catch, Map, Catches, Dashboard, Conditions, and Settings—including how to add an old catch from a photo. You can skip it and replay it anytime from Settings > Help & Feedback > Replay Tour. Settings is behind the profile icon at the top right of every tab. Small tip buttons explain tide percentage, pressure trend, the nearby radius, and Map Center.

Contact

From the app (version 1.2 and later): tap the profile icon (top right), then Settings > Help & Feedback and choose Contact Support, Request a Feature, or Report a Bug. Catch Book opens an email to us with the subject filled in and adds your app version, iOS version, iPhone model, Pro status, and number of catches, so you don’t have to look them up. It never adds your location or catch details, and you can edit anything before you send. If no email app is set up, the app shows our address with a Copy Email button.

From anywhere else: email support@catchbookapp.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the Catch Book version (Settings > About in the app). For backup problems, Settings > Backup & Restore > Where Is My Backup? > Copy These Details and paste them into your email. We never need your password or payment details.
